Review queue

Hiring pipeline

How the village works
  • ←↑↓→ or WASD walk · hold Shift to run
  • Walk into a door to go inside · walk out the bottom to leave
  • T talk to the agent next to you
  • MM (double-tap) talk to your Chief of Staff from anywhere
  • Ask the Chief of Staff to “call Penny” and that director walks over
  • Meeting gathers every director at the Town Hall table
  • G toggle the navigation mesh (green = walkable)
  • Esc close panels
